Twitter Gender Classifier
Classified the gender of Twitter users from their bio, most recent Tweet, and username, comparing logistic regression, an SVM with a Gaussian kernel, and Naive Bayes. Reached 70% accuracy against a published 63%, and wrote up the caveats that limit inferring the gender of anonymous users. Final project for CS 129, with Tara Parekh.

Studied how misfolded proteins, implicated in Alzheimer's, Huntington's, and Parkinson's disease, are sequestered into the juxtanuclear quality control compartment (JUNQ) in S. cerevisiae under proteotoxic stress.
